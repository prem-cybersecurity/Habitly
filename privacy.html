Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Habitly is a habit and goal tracking application designed to help users organize, track and improve their daily habits and goals.

2. Information We Collect

Habitly may collect account information such as your name and email address when you create an account or sign in using Google.

Habit and goal information entered by you may also be stored so that your data can be synchronized across your devices.

3. Google Sign-In

If you use Google Sign-In, Habitly receives basic account information provided by Google, such as your name, email address and profile information, for authentication purposes.

4. Data Storage

Habitly uses Supabase to securely authenticate users and store synchronized application data. Browser storage may also be used as a local cache for offline functionality.

5. Google Drive

If Google Drive functionality is provided and you choose to use it, information is accessed only as necessary for the selected backup or restore functionality.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting the application developer.

8. Contact

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
