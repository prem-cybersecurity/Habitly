Habitly Production Synchronization Hardening

- Google Drive is treated as the persistent source for Drive-enabled users during login.
- User-scoped localStorage is used as a local cache; the legacy generic key is migrated once and removed.
- Stale non-zero `current` values are never promoted into today's daily record.
- Today's progress is derived from daily[today].
- Reminder upcoming dates are calculated from the current date/time instead of stored stale dates.
- Drive file/folder IDs are validated before reuse.
- Existing Drive backups are read before an update; local and remote changes are merged where possible.
- Local changes are tracked at collection level, with daily maps merged for habit records and local deletions respected.
- Concurrent Drive uploads are coalesced into a follow-up upload of the newest state.
- Drive restore blocks automatic upload until initialization is complete.
- Missing remote backups do not silently overwrite a previously meaningful local dataset.
- Drive backup timestamps are only committed after successful upload.
